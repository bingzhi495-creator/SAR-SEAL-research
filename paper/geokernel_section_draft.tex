\subsection{ShapePrior: a geometry-conditioned mask prior for the shape axis}
\label{ssec:shapeprior}
The oracle (Sec.~\ref{ssec:oracle}) localizes a \emph{shape} half of the inshore deficit: the
medium-object mask head-room ($+0.394$ AP$_{75}$) is the larger recoverable pool and is
\emph{not} resolution-limited---the resolution-style refine head is inert on it
(AP$_m$ $-0.2$). Mask2Former produces each mask by a \emph{rank-1} readout,
$M_q=\sigma(\mathbf{e}_q\!\cdot\!E)$, one per-query $256$-vector dotted with a \emph{shared}
feature $E$; it has no per-instance spatial extent, so it cannot synthesize the elongated,
oriented silhouette of a ship and systematically under-segments the low-backscatter
stern/bow where $E\!\approx\!0$. ShapePrior adds, in logit space, a per-instance
\emph{geometry-generated oriented-ellipse prior}, gated:
\begin{equation}
M_q=\sigma\!\big(\underbrace{\mathbf{e}_q\!\cdot\!E}_{\text{rank-1 (kept)}}+\;g_q\,P_q\big),\qquad
P_q=k\,(1-r^2),\;\; r^2=\tfrac{x'^2}{a^2}+\tfrac{y'^2}{b^2},
\label{eq:shapeprior}
\end{equation}
where a tiny head predicts the instance geometry $(\theta,a,b,c_x,c_y)$ and gate $g_q$, and
$(x',y')$ are coordinates rotated by $\theta$ about $(c_x,c_y)$. The prior is a geometry-addressed
\emph{write} into pixel space, so it can fill the hull even where $E$ carries no signal. The gate
is initialised \emph{closed} ($\sigma(-4)\!\approx\!0.018$): the head starts identical to the
rank-1 baseline and must \emph{earn} the prior. We add no resolution and keep the parameter
count fixed (the geometry/gate heads add $0.2\%$ vs.\ the deleted/kept \texttt{mask\_embed}).

\noindent\textbf{Novelty boundary (stated honestly).} Per-instance dynamic mask kernels are
mature (CondInst, SOLOv2, K-Net~\cite{condinst,solov2,knet}); Mask2Former's readout is itself a
rank-1 dynamic kernel; oriented kernels exist for \emph{optical detection}
(ARC~\cite{arc}, on the backbone, not the mask); and ellipse/Gaussian shape priors for SAR ships
exist on the \emph{detection/heatmap} side~\cite{gao2025o2former,sarellipse,mldet,bboxfreesar}.
Our contribution is the conjunction these leave open: a geometry-conditioned prior placed on the
\emph{query-based mask readout}, targeted by a quantitative diagnosis at the medium-shape axis,
and---crucially---\emph{validated by an equal-parameter control}, which no dynamic-kernel or
SAR-shape work runs.

\subsection{Equal-parameter control: geometry vs.\ capacity}
\label{ssec:geocontrol}
The recurring failure mode of dynamic-kernel claims is conflating \emph{conditioning} with added
\emph{capacity}. We isolate them with a four-arm, \emph{parameter-matched, eval-graph-identical}
ladder (all $45{,}171{,}889$ trainable parameters), realised as \texttt{gen\_mode} switches of one
head: \textsc{a0} (gate off $\equiv$ stock Mask2Former), \textsc{static} (one \emph{shared} ellipse,
no per-instance geometry $=$ pure spatial capacity), \textsc{blind} (per-instance free basis masks
with geometry zeroed $=$ content routing), and \textsc{geo} (the full per-query geometry prior).
The decisive quantity is the \emph{delta-of-deltas}: $(\textsc{geo}-\textsc{blind})$ isolates the
geometry effect, while $(\textsc{blind}-\textsc{static})$ measures raw capacity/content.
