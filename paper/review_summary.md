## BLOCKERS

### B1 [Experiments §main / Method §SealM2FHead / Abstract — equal-parameter control] (conf=high)
**Issue**: The paper's own ethos is 'minimal surgery on the diagnosed axis, not added capacity', yet the equal-parameter control that would test exactly this is marked \TODO{not yet run}. SealM2FHead adds learned parameters (U upsampler + R_theta residual conv). Without a parameter-matched control that adds the same capacity WITHOUT the 1/4->1/2 resolution change (or adds R_theta at 1/4 stride), the +2.6 cannot be distinguished from 'the extra capacity bought 2.6 AP75'. The bilinear-only control isolates resolution-vs-learning but does NOT isolate capacity-vs-mechanism: A0R-bilinear still increases resolution, so it cannot serve as the capacity control. This is the single most important missing control given the stated positioning.

**Fix**: Run a parameter-matched control: e.g. (a) R_theta applied at 1/4 stride (same params, no resolution lift) and/or (b) an extra conv block of equal parameter count added to the stock final layer. Report its AP75 delta. If it recovers <<+2.6, the resolution-axis claim survives; if comparable, downgrade to 'capacity on the final layer helps' and re-frame. Until run, the abstract/intro must explicitly flag that the gain is not yet separated from added capacity, not just from resolution.

### B2 [Abstract / Intro / Method §sec:oracle / Experiments §ssec:oracle] (conf=high)
**Issue**: The '60% detection / 40% mask-resolution' decomposition is the spine of the whole paper, yet (a) it is numerically inflated and (b) the 'mask-resolution oracle' it rests on was never run. The only source is runs/a0_pseg_seed0/mask_quality_oracle.json (inshore/*: det_rate 0.657, recoverable_ap75_frac 0.233). The script computes the split as rec/(rec+miss) = 0.233/(0.233+0.343) = 40.5% resolution / 59.5% detection. Rounding 40.5→40 and 59.5→60 is defensible arithmetic, but this ratio is a count of detected GTs whose mask-IoU falls in [0.5,0.75) divided by a count of misses — it is NOT a decomposition of the AP75 deficit, and the two counts are not commensurable units (a 'recoverable AP75 fraction' vs a 'detection miss rate'). Worse, both Method and Experiments describe a different, BPR-style oracle: 'an oracle that substitutes ground-truth labels for pixels near the predicted boundary of correctly-detected instances and measures the recovered AP75 ... that grows for smaller objects.' No such experiment exists anywhere in runs/; mask_quality_oracle.py performs no label substitution and reports no recovered AP75. This is an overclaim of an experiment not performed. The 'grows for smaller objects' claim is directly contradicted by the JSON: inshore recoverable_frac is S=0.192 < M=0.333 (it shrinks for smaller objects).

**Fix**: Either (1) actually run the boundary-patch oracle (substitute GT pixels in a band around detected-instance boundaries, re-run COCOeval, report the real recovered AP75 by size) and report whatever it gives; or (2) drop all 'oracle that substitutes labels and measures recovered AP75' language and rename this to what it is — a 'detected-but-sub-AP75 mask-quality audit'. State the decomposition as '~41% of inshore GTs that ARE detected fall short of AP75 on mask quality, vs ~34% undetected', and explicitly flag that these are instance-count shares, not an AP-additive decomposition. Delete 'grows for smaller objects'. Soften Abstract/Intro to '~60/40' only if the count-share framing is made explicit.

### B3 [Method §sec:mne / Experiments §ssec:negatives (G1) / Abstract] (conf=high)
**Issue**: The MNE 'honest negative' is selectively framed and reads as more honest than it is. The draft states MNE 'does not beat a box-filter z-score by a meaningful margin (±0.002)'. But the actual gate (runs/g1_full, g1_hrsid) is against the STRONGEST classical control, ts_cfar, where mne_E LOSES by −0.0123 (PSeg) and −0.0055 (HRSID), and against box_raw which beats everything on HRSID (0.9915). Reporting only the comparison to the weak box_z control (where the gap is a flattering ±0.002) is exactly the cherry-picking the 'honest reporting' ethos forbids. Separately and more damaging to the 'not a discriminator' thesis: the fusion variant mne_full is the single best method INSHORE on PSeg (0.9597 AUROC, +7.6pt over box_z, +0.8-1.0pt over ts_cfar) — i.e. a multi-scale evidence channel DOES discriminate inshore, the paper just chose to deploy the single rank channel. The draft never mentions mne_full, so the headline negative ('MNE is not a discriminator') is true only of the weaker variant the authors elected to ship.

**Fix**: Report G1 against the strongest control, not the weakest: state 'mne_E does not beat the strongest classical control ts_cfar (−0.012 PSeg / −0.006 HRSID) on per-pixel AUROC'. Disclose mne_full's inshore +7.6pt result and explain why the single rank channel is shipped anyway (cross-task sharing / zero-param / margin still below the pre-registered +0.03 gate). This makes the negative genuinely honest rather than honest-against-a-strawman. If mne_full's inshore edge is omitted, a reviewer who reads the gate verdicts will conclude the negative was framed to look like principled humility while hiding a positive result.
