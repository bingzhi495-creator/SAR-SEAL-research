All 14 manuscript pages and all 3 supplementary pages were rendered and viewed. Independent reviewers covered manuscript pages 1–7 and 8–14 plus S1–S3; the lead reviewed the relocated overview, final enlarged empirical figures, and last bibliography page.

- No page-edge clipping, broken formulas, missing glyphs, obstructed result cells, or detached captions were identified.

- Main Figs. 5 and 7 were enlarged to 85% of text width for readable empirical labels. Existing figure-label aliases for NDM/OAEM reimplementations are made explicit in the caption. TP/FP values near dense overlays are repeated in the caption.
