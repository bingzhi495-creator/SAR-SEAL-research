Consensus-gated vote fusion illustrated by a deterministic replay on the 16 highest-scoring stored candidates from one HRSID inshore chip. Top: sampled TTA overlays and the pairwise mask IoU of the same 16 masks, reordered by score-seeded greedy clustering (white: zero; blue: one). Solid vermilion blocks mark supports in the voting band; dotted gray blocks mark the other supports. (a) The six-member subset cluster is pixel-voted. (b) The seven-member subset cluster keeps its seed; overlaying the seed contour on the rejected majority mask reveals the hull pixels that voting would remove. Both panels use $110\times86$ native-pixel viewports at 22.58\,mm width, preserving the same magnification. The left edge in (a) is the actual image boundary. This top-16 replay illustrates the operator; it is not the full 800-candidate deployment, where these two seeds collect 10 and 9 members, respectively, and both keep the seed.
