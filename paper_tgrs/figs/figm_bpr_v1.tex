% figm_bpr.pdf — boundary-patch refinement (BPR) pipeline.
% Training (left): full-stack predictions on the train split are matched to GT
% at mask IoU>=0.5; 64x64 patches sampled along the matched boundary, 2x
% upsampled, refined by a 2.35M UNet (4 down / 4 up) under BCE+Dice.
% Inference (right): +-4 px band (dilate - erode) around the predicted
% boundary; UNet re-segments patches, overlapping logits are averaged inside
% the band, thresholded at 0.5; edits confined to the band. Banner: scores
% untouched, main model untouched.
% Facts: ALL_TABLES.md T1 row 4, T10 (audit line); sec_method.tex III-E.
