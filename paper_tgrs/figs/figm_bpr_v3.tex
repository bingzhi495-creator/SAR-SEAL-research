% figm_bpr.pdf — optional boundary-patch refinement (Sec. III-E).
% Left  (training, once, ~19 min): full-stack predictions on the TRAIN split
%   matched to GT at mask IoU >= 0.5; 64x64 patches are cut FROM the contour of
%   the 800x800 mask -- drawn at true scale, 1.6 mm against the mask's 20 mm --

%   article-wide scale of 1 mm per 40 px holds multiplied by 4.
% Right (inference): the +-4 px band is 1% of an 800 px side, so it is shown in
%   an explicit x8 zoom on the contour: the annulus is the only writable region
%   and both the interior and the exterior carry lock glyphs.
% Facts: sec_method.tex III-E, Eqs. (bprloss)/(guard); ALL_TABLES.md T1 row 4,
%   T2 (+BPR rows), T10 (inshore AP50 +0.7 against AP75 +10.9).
