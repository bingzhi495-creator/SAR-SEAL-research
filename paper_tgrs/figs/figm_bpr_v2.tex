% figm_bpr.pdf — optional boundary-patch refinement (Sec. III-E).
% Left  (training, once, ~19 min): full-stack predictions on the TRAIN split
%   matched to GT at mask IoU >= 0.5; 64x64 patches cropped along each matched
%   contour, upscaled 2x, used to fit a 2.35M UNet under BCE + Dice.
% Right (inference): a +-4 px band b(p) around the predicted contour is the
%   only editable region; the UNet re-segments the patches, overlapping logits
%   are averaged inside the band and thresholded at 0.5; every pixel outside
%   is bit-identical and no score, rank or identity is touched.
