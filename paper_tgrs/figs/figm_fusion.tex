%   * candidate pool -- tensorpile of 3 planes, front face = the REAL view 3

%     candidates, permuted into greedy-cluster order, assets/iou_matrix.png,
%     608 px over 25.0 mm = 618 dpi.  Off-block IoU is EXACTLY 0, i.e. the
%     four objects are disjoint -- the white L-shaped field is a measurement.
%   * the four diagonal blocks are drawn from cluster_ranges of
%     assets/iou_matrix_meta.json, [0,5] [6,12] [13,13] [14,15], with cell

%       block(a,b) = x in [3.85+a*0.15625, 3.85+(b+1)*0.15625],
%                    y in [6.77-(b+1)*0.15625, 6.77-a*0.15625].
%     Two styles, and the distinction is a measurement, not a decoration:
%     |C| = 6 and 2 fall inside the deployed band 2 <= |C| <= 6 (solid
%     vermillion 0.8 pt), |C| = 7 and 1 fall outside (dotted grey 0.5 pt).
%   * the gate is an ARROW FORK, not a diamond: one trunk leaving the
%     clustering operator, two labelled arms, each landing on its panel.
%
% Bottom row (what the two branches do to real pixels):
%   (a) the in-band cluster |C| = 6 -- assets/fusion/inband6/, dumped crop
%       266 px, shown through a 118 px window (see D5), 19.25 mm, 623 dpi.
%       members_contours -> vote_count_color -> fused.  211 pixels are
%       contested; the graded blue ring in the vote map is the real
%       per-pixel support count, 1 at the rim and 6 in the core.
%   (b) the out-of-band cluster |C| = 7 -- assets/fusion/outband7/, native
%       crop 110 px, drawn at 19.25 mm, 880 px = 1161 dpi.
%       members_contours -> seed kept -> the mask a majority vote WOULD have
%       produced, reached by a dashed "path not taken" arrow under a cross.
%   Both panels are therefore at essentially ONE magnification (19.25 mm over
%   118 px and over 110 px), so the two ships may be compared by eye: the
%   in-band instance really is the smaller object.
%
% THE ONE PIECE OF EVIDENCE THE WHOLE FIGURE EXISTS FOR is the (b) pair.  To
% make the difference visible rather than asserted, the seed's own contour is
% stroked in vermillion on top of the counterfactual crop: the white region
% falls away from that line along the upper-left hull, which is the stretch a
% majority rule would have shaved off.  The polygon is not drawn by hand -- it
% is the exact pixel-centre boundary of assets/fusion/outband7/seed.png,

%   x = 16.60 - 0.9625 + (col+0.5)/110*1.925 ,
%   y =  1.75 + 0.9625 - (row+0.5)/110*1.925 ,

%  D1 the four cluster sizes are set immediately LEFT of the matrix, aligned
%     with each block's row span, instead of on the diagonal blocks.  A 7 pt
%     digit is 0.13 cm; blocks 3 and 4 are 0.156 and 0.313 cm wide, so a digit

%     forbids glyph-on-raster ink.  Row alignment keeps the reading (rows are
%     candidates) and puts no ink on data.
%  D2 the fork is routed as one trunk down from the clustering block and two
%     horizontal arms at y = 3.50, rather than the two right-going stubs of
%     the spec's geometry table, which ended in mid-air at x = 10.60 and

%  D3 "seeds in score order, tau=0.5" measures 3.1 cm in 7 pt Times, so the

%  D4 no per-panel container frames: one dotted rule at x = 9.10 separates the
