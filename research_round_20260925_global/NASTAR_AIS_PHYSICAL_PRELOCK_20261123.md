# Frozen external physical-dimension evaluation on NASTaR

Test whether freeing distance pairing at identical signed edit counts lowers error in the estimated length of a vessel along its reported true heading. NASTaR supplies NovaSAR S-band images and AIS dimensions. Nominal spatial resolution is 6 m and the image sample spacing is approximately 2.5 m. AIS provides a reference point, typically the antenna. Its dimensions are declared vessel dimensions and can differ from the observed radar footprint. The reported AIS matching window is five minutes around acquisition. This experiment measures paired dimension errors on center-referenced vessel chips.
