# Paired supervision test of non-half-cell occupancies

The earlier D-FINE source-group experiment gave fractional targets +0.184 mask AP over COCO rasters, but only +0.051 over binarizing the same occupancies at 0.50. Exactly 2,924 of its 4,167 fractional cells were half covered. That result is consistent with some advantage coming from the treatment of half-covered cells or rasterization alignment. Novelty in soft supervision as a general principle is outside this experiment. SoftSeg used non-binary training for partial volume in medical image segmentation. BoundaryFormer used differentiable mask-space supervision for polygon boundaries. Here the labels come from exact intersections with the original SAR raster cells, and the comparison isolates which occupancy values matter for a frozen local editor.
