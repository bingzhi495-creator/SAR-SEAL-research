# Crossed Phoenix and SAR occupancy ordering at fixed signed counts

## Question and cohort

On 74 already evaluated Instance-RSDD chips, adapted Phoenix ordering scored 0.308 AP points above the mean of the three frozen occupancy heads at identical edit counts, with the fixed-mask continuous IoU confidence interval crossing zero. Determine whether at equal support and counts the two orders differ on additions, deletions, or both. A small total geometry difference can arise from either weak signed effects or signed effects of opposite direction. This is an exposed-cohort diagnostic. Nothing in the new scoring selects a target policy, model, threshold, budget, split or fitted hypothesis. [Frozen equal-count result](INSTANCE_RSDD_PHOENIX_EQUAL_COUNT_ACTUAL_20261123.md) · [Classical control](INSTANCE_RSDD_CLASSICAL_FAIR_MATCH_ACTUAL_20261123.md)

Preserve the original 74 chips, 81 hulls, 13 parents, 7,346 candidates, scores, boxes, source RGB and unchanged native fallback. Use the previously accepted raw detector, 4,107 positive-count candidates, exact signed endpoint sets and dynamic realized swap count k for every candidate. Each edited policy performs exactly 29,900 additions and 29,900 removals over the whole cohort. All source scores, edits and learned weights remain frozen. No new author model call, point-head call, detector forward or optimizer update. Phoenix refers only to the same native-endpoint adapted ranking, which bypassed the COCO/Cityscapes one-percent gate. The same Phoenix reference is reused across the three frozen occupancy seeds.

## Four cells per occupancy seed

Let P denote the archived Phoenix order and L denote a fixed occupancy seed (5217, 5218 or 5219). The first letter always selects added positions; the second letter selects removed positions. Retain the original P/P and each L/L mask, and use their exact signed RLE differences relative to the same stock mask to create L/P and P/L. Preserve the original raw, dynamic, static, Phoenix and three occupancy arms byte-identically. Require the observed label-free replay, full candidate order, old edit projection and per-side count/sign equivalence before opening author labels. Recheck the complete old arm metrics on all admitted views after scoring.

Assess each edited policy by independent COCO rematching. Report AP, AP75, AP90, small-target AP and AR100 of all cells, paired side differences and the four-cell interaction. An AP interaction can reflect threshold and matching changes. It is an estimated response of the whole fixed candidate set. For fixed geometry, preserve the original 0.50 score-ordered native mask matches, area filter and valid continuous polygons, including unchanged comparisons. At each candidate/seed, compare polygon intersections in px². Require the interaction I(LL)−I(LP)−I(PL)+I(PP) to be within 1e−7 px² before bootstrap. Report true intersection as percent of author polygon area, and false negative, false positive and continuous IoU as parallel diagnostics. With fixed mask area, signs of the IoU and intersection change agree for each matched instance.

## Primary inference

Use the preexisting full 74 view as primary. The only two primary sides are occupancy additions (LP−PP) and occupancy removals (PL−PP), both as percentage-point differences in normalized intersection, equal weighting the parent scenes represented in the fixed geometry. For each side, average the paired effects of three occupancy seeds within each parent before resampling, while also reporting each seed effect. Use 4,096 paired bootstrap draws with NumPy PCG64 seed 5217 and sensor/date blocks, shared draws for both contrasts. Use equal-tailed 97.5% intervals, a Bonferroni adjustment across the two primary sides for 95% familywise confidence. Declare a directional side result only if the adjusted interval excludes zero, the three seed effect estimates have the same corresponding strict sign and at least 20 fixed hulls across eight date blocks have a different position set on that side in all three seeds. Include parent-only sensitivity and the existing date-separated 42-chip and separate sensor views as secondary. Retain all sign counts, exposure and confidence intervals.

Neither a positive geometry side nor a favorable hybrid AP establishes a new deployment rule. This experiment identifies which frozen rank differs at a shared boundary and budget, or records that direction unresolved. Any source-trained hybrid must be trained and frozen in a separate source-only stage with independent external confirmation.
