## Evaluation and decision

The full 74-image view is primary, with 42-date-separated, both sensor views and existing deduplicated view secondary. Reuse independent COCO rematching and fixed native area-qualified mask matches for continuous geometry from the closed classical run, with equal source parent weights and 4,096 paired sensor/date block bootstrap draws at seed 5217. Give AP, AP75, AR100 and normalized pixel FP/FN alongside IoU. Report each occupancy seed minus Phoenix, equal-weight seed average and a fixed-geometry 95% interval, and report all comparisons to archived controls without picking a best seed. A directional mean is insufficient alone. Advance the learned-order advantage over Phoenix only if all three AP differences are strictly positive, the seed-mean fixed IoU interval has lower bound >0, and at least 20 eligible fixed matches across at least eight sensor/date blocks have distinct positions for all three seeds versus Phoenix. For a symmetric Phoenix advantage, require all three AP differences strictly negative and the upper IoU bound <0 with the same exposure gate. Otherwise keep comparisons descriptive or conditional.
