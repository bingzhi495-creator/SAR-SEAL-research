Title
NORA: Native-Grid Occupancy Ranking and Allocation for SAR Ship Instance Segmentation

Abstract
A small boundary shift can substantially reduce overlap for a narrow ship in synthetic aperture radar (SAR) imagery. We introduce Native-Grid Occupancy Ranking and Allocation (NORA), a mask editor whose residual occupancy head (ROH) scores hull occupancy at native boundary cells. At the same per-side counts, independent selection improves mask average precision (AP) by 0.81–0.91 points over distance-first pairing on HRSID DEV705. On the historical 74-chip Instance-RSDD reserve, it improves AP by 0.479 and 0.344 points over bounded re-pairing with frozen RF-DETR and D-FINE pipelines. Continuous intersection over union (IoU) improves by 0.116 and 0.133 points, respectively. With RF-DETR, bounded re-pairing increases one-pixel Boundary IoU by 0.735 points over distance-first pairing. The paired 95% interval for the further change under independent selection spans zero. When only the total edit count is fixed, varying the addition–removal split gives mean gains of 2.30 and 1.75 AP points across three fits of the dual-reference ROH on the same SSDD images under two annotation releases. Frozen transfer to SAR-SSP reduces missed area, increases false foreground, and lowers AP by 0.14 points.

Index Terms
Synthetic aperture radar, ship instance segmentation, boundary refinement, native image grid
