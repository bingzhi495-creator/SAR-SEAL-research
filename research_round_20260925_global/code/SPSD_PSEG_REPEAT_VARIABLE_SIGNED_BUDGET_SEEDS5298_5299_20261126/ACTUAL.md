# Repeat validation of fixed-total variable signed mask edits

**Completed 26 November 2026.** Both independent BCE fits reproduced the matched advantage from asymmetric signed allocation on this source-development cohort. Seed 5298 passed, seed 5299 passed, and the prespecified two-repeat pool passed the full advancement rule.

## Matched design

The repeat fits used the same FIT packet, candidate-balanced dual BCE objective, initial model family, steps and predictor inputs at independent seeds 5298 and 5299. Each new head received 1,000 updates. An additional current-runtime 5297 verification checked exact repeatability against same-runtime probes. The original archived 5297 head and its outcomes supplied the closed historical result. The archived and current repeatable 5297 tensors are distinct historical/runtime artifacts.

Each repeat held all original 183 source-development images, their 180 decoded groups, all 18,300 native candidates, scores, quotas, support coordinates and archive masks. For each of 8,785 positive slots, every alternative made exactly 2k allowed flips, preserving the total 115,384 flips. New same-seed BCE robust/mean/cell-pessimistic comparisons used the exact same head and k/k restriction. The identical finite source-locked action solver supplied the estimated-IoU fixed (k/k), variable, and near-fixed writers and the two variable objective controls. All exact source, mask and budget assertions ran before both independent author references were opened. Every old mask metric was recovered exactly.

The predeclared confirmation requires each repeat separately and the two-repeat pool to meet both advancement comparisons. Each uses +0.15 fixed-match IoU points, positive 95% paired lower under both labels, AP noninferiority ≥−0.10 under both, and at least 20 changed common positive-quota hulls in eight groups. Positive means versus same-seed BCE mean and static are also required for both labels. The group resampling keeps the two reference variants and all seed outcomes from a decoded group together. Pooled AP averages per-seed AP-point differences, while pooled IoU averages paired group differences by seed before 4,096 shared resamples.
