Earlier SSDD experiments used matched polygon IoU and threshold counts to supervise quota choice. They achieved a large truth quota opportunity and failed to exceed the best fixed ratio with a deployable head. Quota supervision here uses the real score-ordered competition in the original full candidate pool, including candidates without an original native match. It measures a unilateral effect at the balanced mask configuration. Applying a learned policy to every candidate can change the effects through interaction and requires a separately frozen full-cohort evaluation. This phase has no new training, hidden test, or external dataset access. It reuses source images previously seen by the frozen detector.
