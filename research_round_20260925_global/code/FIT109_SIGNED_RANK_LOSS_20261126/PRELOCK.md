# FIT109 matched 200-update comparison of within-side rank supervision

Test whether an occupancy-gap-weighted rank term improves fixed-budget endpoint selection beyond matched BCE and the original independent writer. Earlier work used a single seed and 1,000 updates, reporting +0.172 mask AP and +0.082 continuous IoU for BCE plus same-side ranking over BCE under that protocol. Here test the deployed head's original 200-step scale with three seeds and matched sampling. Pairwise rank supervision is established. The question is its measured effect on this fractional native-cell edit problem. [Earlier FIT109 study](../../FIT109_POINT_RANKING_AND_DECISION_SUPERVISION_20261121.md) · [Rank-based detector supervision](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/ICCV2021/html/Oksuz_Rank__Sort_Loss_for_Object_Detection_and_Instance_Segmentation_ICCV_2021_paper.html) · [Posterior ranking for IoU](https://www.jmlr.org/papers/v24/22-0712.html)

## Source and matching

Use the previously witnessed RF-DETR center5202 FIT109 capture, original 109 HRSID images, 10,488 native candidates, 6,421 original endpoint supports, source-prefix fold assignment from the original fit plan, and their canonical fractional polygon-cell truth. Use only the original FIT reference. Hold all observations from the assigned prefix fold out of point-head training and standardization. The RF-DETR detector previously saw these images and source prefixes are acquisition proxies. This is cross-fitted point-head validation on a historically evaluated source set. DEV705, SRNet, SAR-SSP, and Umbra are outside this run.

Train B (fractional BCE) and R (fractional BCE plus within-side rank loss) on the original 1,321-parameter scalar PointHead with 14 native features and shared partner layout. For seeds 5217, 5218, 5219 and each of five original folds, keep one identical initial model, 200 AdamW updates, learning rate .002, weight decay .01, original training-fold-only equal-prefix feature moments and single-thread CPU numerical model. Draw 16 training prefixes uniformly with replacement per update, one truth-valid candidate uniformly in each drawn prefix, and four original pair rows with replacement within each candidate. Use PCG64(20261112 + seed − 5217) and the same exact packets across both arms in each fold. Both arms use the same 64 paired rows and 128 target cells. This local grouping makes within-candidate rank comparisons possible under the 64-pair budget.

In each four-row packet, take all six unordered row pairs on each of the two sides. For occupancies t_a and t_b and logits z_a and z_b, the rank term per pair is abs(t_a−t_b)*softplus(−sign(t_a−t_b)*(z_a−z_b)). Average over the fixed 192 comparisons in the update, with ties contributing zero. R adds 0.25 times that mean to the same BCE as B. The label gap weights potential intersection mass at equal signed counts. Keep the same input/scaler, prior, weights architecture, seed, sampler, and optimizer beyond this intervention. Log exact updates, schedule digests, parameter count and wall time. Do not choose loss weights or fit epochs from outcomes.

## Frozen edit and evaluation

Replay every held source image and candidate in its original order. For each seed and original fold, use the corresponding archived deployed head F to set that exact candidate's realized k. Score both new heads by independent endpoints with k additions and k removals, using the original FP32 order, admissible endpoint pool and original tie order. Keep score, native mask identity, no-op slots and total mask area. Include archived N, distance-first F and independent I as context with the same k. Reproduce archive seed 5217 fixed counts 6,310 positive candidates and 46,581 exchanges per side, plus original AP and all-hull geometry under the canonical same-support protocol. Require observed training closure and an exact independent label-free replay readback before opening the scoring annotation input. Training label use is restricted to each fold.

For each seed use unchanged full COCO mask scoring and report mask AP, AP75, AP90, APs, AR100 and available other official areas. Report full original 496-hull fixed discrete geometry including unmatched zero contributions, plus original eligible fixed-match continuous IoU from the fractional mask geometry, misses/excess area, changed candidates and supported anchors. For aggregate effects average seed-wise pooled AP differences. For geometry average shared-anchor effects across the three heads, then use parent/prefix-equal means and a shared 4,096-draw paired original prefix bootstrap with PCG64(5217). Report all individual seeds and denominators. A meaningful positive comparison needs at least +0.30 average AP points, strictly positive mean blocked 95% continuous-IoU interval, positive AP in each of the three seeds, and changed positive-k eligible matches at least 20 across at least eight original prefixes. Apply a symmetric negative rule. Evaluate R−B and R−I separately. Advancing this source recipe requires both predeclared positive contrasts. Report inconclusive estimates even if criteria fail. This run cannot provide prospective external validation.
