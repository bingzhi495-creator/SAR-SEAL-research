## Decision

The primary contrast is I−L and the explanatory contrast is L−F. A directional comparison qualifies if the absolute mask AP point estimate is at least 0.30, the paired 95% continuous-IoU interval lies strictly on the same side of zero, and at least 20 eligible original matched hulls across at least eight groups differ. Apply the symmetric loss convention. L recovers the original improvement within practical tolerances when L−F qualifies as positive and the I−L AP point estimate lies within ±0.15 and its entire continuous-IoU interval lies within ±0.15. Treat these as descriptive AP tolerances and a paired geometry interval. Report mixed results and each separate scope. The nested 42-chip sensitivity and multiple fits on the same chips cannot increase the count of independent acquisitions.
