# Held-out FIT source-group three-seed area signal

The sole scientific question is whether the median area of three independently updated, shared-initialization HRSID center models predicts the anchor's source polygon area residual better than ordinary alternatives. The earlier truth-assisted local edit oracle showed equal-effort capacity, and the previous association audit showed ordinary ranked matching can recover many uniquely identified vessels. This test uses 36 new image-only FIT chips from prefixes disjoint from both prior 36-prefix cohorts. Exclude FIT114 image identities and same-prefix intersecting crop rectangles. Earlier FIT114 includes these mother-prefix proxies on other chips and the three detectors were trained on FIT. No source-independent model generalization claim.

Stage one is label-free. Reuse frozen natural-retention center E24, seeds 5201, 5202 and 5203, with 5202 anchor; original CPU FP32 val576, same three forward costs, all native top-100 query ranks and unchanged masks/scores. In each seed suppress near-duplicate masks by ordinary native-rank greedy mask NMS at IoU >= .90 for association only. Match [32,1024) anchor representatives to one available donor per side, stock rank first, choose max IoU >= .50 on each side with stable native tie, no reuse and no consumption if either side missing. No AP, edits, detector training or label access. Minimum stage-one coverage stays >=60 eligible anchors across >=20 groups and >=30 nonzero median offsets across >=15 groups. Seal three candidate streams and signed predictions before FIT annotation access. Failure stops scoring under this protocol.

Use the same covered-anchor gate and area change cap C=2*min(8, floor(A0/10)) for every fitted arm. Use three areas including the anchor for arithmetic mean minus A0 and median minus A0. The native-only soft control is sum of sigmoid(native interpolated mask logit) minus original foreground occupancy in the two-pixel Chebyshev boundary band (dilation minus erosion). Native bilinear align_corners=false interpolation and >0 threshold must reconstruct the stock anchor mask exactly. The other native-only size feature is C*log2(A0/sqrt(32*1024))/2.5. Signals and association stay frozen even when some donor identities later remain unresolved.

Stage two requires exact naturally closed stage-one readback and COVERAGE_PASS. Original raw nonempty stock COCO .50 matches on the same FIT images define the eligible denominator once per valid GT. Ground truth is the original source continuous polygon area. Match identity never changes by arm; include every eligible matched candidate, with uncovered unchanged in every arm. Use three source group folds by locked hash-ordered ordinal modulo 3. Fit affine bounded residual d=clip(C*a+b*x,-C,C) separately to each of the four inputs on the other two folds, with intercept grid [0,-.5,.5,-1,1] and slope grid [0,.25,.50,.75,1,1.25,1.50,1.75,2]; ties follow this written order. Fit with equal source-group then match weights. Include zero change. Primary held group-macro mean of absolute area error normalized by continuous truth area over full fixed matched denominator. Report covered and pooled summaries, held fold differences and calibration, and unmatched/neutral changed candidates; predicted area only, no pixel edit.

A lead requires >=30 covered valid matched vessels from >=15 source groups, median improving >=0.50 normalized-MAE percentage points versus zero and >=0.25 versus each calibrated mean, native soft and native size, with favorable sign against each in at least two of three held folds. No CI guarantee in this internal cross-fit. Positive only authorizes a separately locked downstream mask allocation test. Negative keeps all controls and stops this median mechanism at this support. No DEV, TEST, AP or optimizer updates in the three-seed branch.
