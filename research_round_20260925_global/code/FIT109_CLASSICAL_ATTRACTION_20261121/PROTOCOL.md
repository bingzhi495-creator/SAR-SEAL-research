# HRSID FIT109 matched-count spatial-attraction comparison

Atkinson's pixel-swapping rule ranks opposite-sign sites by spatial attraction and preserves foreground counts. It operates on sub-pixels within a coarse pixel and recomputes attraction after edits. This test adapts that rule to the already fixed endpoints of native SAR instance masks. The goal is to compare a learned occupancy signal with a classical geometry-only signal. [Original algorithm, p. 841, Eqs. 2–6](https://www.asprs.org/wp-content/uploads/pers/2005journal/jul/2005_jul_839-846.pdf#page=3)

Use the closed 10,900 D-FINE E24 candidates, original FIT109 source folds and frozen point features. Replay the original five held-out BCE occupancy predictors with 200 updates per fold and zero detector forwards. All original masks, candidate identity, rank, box, score, endpoint sets, and native-grid labels stay fixed.

Use binary foreground attraction with Euclidean radius 5 pixels and weight exp(−distance/5), excluding the center. The radius and decay scale follow the numeric real-image setting in the source. A disk on the native grid, zero exterior, fixed endpoint eligibility and no repeated endpoint flips are adaptations. Independently select the largest available addition attraction and smallest removal attraction and update after an accepted swap. Stop at a margin of at most 10⁻¹² or the common candidate cap min(8, floor(P/10), endpoint count). Resolve ties within 10⁻¹² by original per-side endpoint ordinal. The adaptation uses the source rule's difference of attractions, which does not include an interaction term between the two selected sites.

The main pair is learned independent ranking at the exact realized dynamic-attraction edit count versus dynamic attraction. Static attraction at the same count provides a controlled ranking comparison and isolates the role of recomputing attraction from the initial ranking. Report both learned-natural and dynamic-attraction natural counts separately. The originally reported raw, fixed-pair, learned same-count and learned natural controls must replay exactly. Audit per-mask equal counts, signs, distinct endpoints and total XOR size. Include unsupported masks and zero edits.

After the run, report COCO mask AP, AP75 and AP90, all 412 original valid fixed matches with 93 source groups, and continuous IoU point differences with paired 4,096-draw group intervals. Report learned minus dynamic, learned minus static, and dynamic minus static. Preserve both signs and the realized counts. For fixed-match transitions, this D-FINE FIT109 reproduction uses the inherited inclusive threshold of 0.90. This is a historical source-side comparison. The detector saw these images, while the learned occupancy heads hold out sources by fold. The comparison leaves the original decisions, current official HRSID protocol and transfer evidence intact.
