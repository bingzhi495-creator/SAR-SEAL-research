# SSDD DEV187 paired along-hull and across-hull subpixel shift

Reuse the four independently accepted prediction streams and the 380 exact integer/official-float label pairs. Fix a thin-object population using the minimum-area rectangle of each original continuous integer polygon, aspect ratio at least 2. The long edge defines the tangent and its perpendicular defines the normal. Orient each axis toward the projection of the original official-float minus integer centroid offset, with a deterministic coordinate tie rule. Score unchanged continuous integer shapes and symmetric 0.5-pixel translations on the two axes. Keep shape, area, metadata, candidates, scores and thresholds fixed. Make all objects outside the thin population or leaving the canvas in any translated cell neutral throughout. Report original annotation/size support, actual projected shift magnitudes, analytic original-versus-shifted self IoU, continuous mask AP and all diagnostic metrics in five fixed cells. Primary comparison is half the center-minus-native AP response between the forward and backward shifts on each axis, and the across minus along difference. Also report symmetric-average responses. Predict larger negative across-axis odd response than along-axis in both seeds. The equal magnitudes distinguish directional sensitivity from the size of the natural projection. This is fixed-prediction label sensitivity and does not establish the behavior of new training.
