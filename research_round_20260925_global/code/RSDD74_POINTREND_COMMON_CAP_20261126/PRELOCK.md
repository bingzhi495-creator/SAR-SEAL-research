# PointRend posterior crossover at the original equal signed count and edge cap

## Question

Test whether the benefit of independent signed endpoint ranking over exact bounded pairing survives a change of point scorer. The learned occupancy head already gives +0.479 mask AP and +0.116 fixed-match continuous IoU points against exact bounded re-pairing on the 74-chip reserve, with no established one-pixel boundary advantage over the same control. The previous same-support PointRend comparison changed the scorer under independent selection at a different natural-count budget. It did not estimate the writer effect at a common displacement cap for both frozen scores. PointRend uses iterative subdivision to predict local mask detail. We reuse the accepted RF-DETR adaptation and its propagated scores, distinct from an evaluation of the unmodified Detectron2 system. ## Frozen crossover

Use the existing historical 74-chip Instance-RSDD reserve and original nested date-separated 42 chips, with the closed RF-DETR 5202 detector, original 1,321-parameter occupancy head 5217 and original RF-DETR PointRend adaptation. Keep all 7,346 native candidate slots, confidence, boxes, rankings, per-candidate source endpoint coordinates and native sign. Reconstruct the original PointRend head and posterior calculation from the already captured features and unchanged learned weights. Prove original matched-support PointRend masks at the previous 31,391-swap budget are exact before accepting the new PointRend scores. There are no new detector forwards or optimizer steps, no data or checkpoint selection and no new external acquisition.

For the new crossover, inherit the original distance-first occupancy F per-candidate k and r² (maximum squared displacement of its k accepted original pairs). Check the 4,092 positive candidates and 30,841 additions plus 30,841 removals per variant. The old PointRend budget is used solely for parity and is distinct from this budget. Use the same r² for both scores even if the adapted PointRend would select a different set of paired rows. Leave all zero-budget masks unchanged.

At each scorer h, F uses k highest gain among the same original assignment rows whose squared distance is at most r², with stable row order for ties. The original occupancy F is the archived arm and must remain byte-identical. L maximizes the exact sum of signed endpoint scores over k disjoint pairs with squared edge length at most r², using the released exact integer solver. I independently ranks the two sides at k, with stable original row order on posterior ties for the new crossover. The F arm supplies a feasible witness for L under each scorer. Require exact objective ordering J_h(F) <= J_h(L) <= J_h(I), signed support, cap, k/k etc. Verify the learned F/L/I archives and every original candidate reference before applying the new PointRend scores. Use the original native pixel tie convention to reproduce the older PointRend archive at its distinct budget, and record the scope of tie conventions. The within-scorer comparison changes the writer only. Between scores, network inputs, architecture, training and inference costs may differ.

## Evaluation and decision

Confirm witnessed closure and independent reconstruction of the PointRend swaps and masks before reading the agreed historical author reference. Recover original learned variants and unchanged N and preserve all original variant confidence/ordering. Recompute COCO mask AP, AP50, AP75, AP90, APsmall and AR100 on all original candidates. Freeze original raster IoU 0.50 matches and valid polygon/[32,1024) geometry rules, with full 81 and nested 47 author annotations, 47 and 32 eligible matches, 12 and 8 contributing parents, and 11 and 8 sensor–date blocks. Use exact continuous unit-cell intersection, and report fixed-match IoU, FP/FN and changed matches/groups. Parent-weighted point effects and 4,096 paired sensor–date resamples retain equal parent weighting. Reuse the previously published one- and two-pixel native boundary definition as mechanism sensitivities on the same fixed matches. Intervals estimate acquisition variation among these fixed fits.

The primary contrast is PointRend I minus PointRend L on the full 74-chip view. The effect qualifies as positive at >=+0.30 mask AP points, a strictly positive 95% fixed-match continuous-IoU lower bound, and at least 20 changed eligible matches from eight contributing sensor–date blocks. Use symmetric criteria for a negative result. Report the 42-chip view, PointRend L minus F, and all learned comparisons regardless. The second contrast estimates the writer-by-score interaction, (occupancy I minus L) minus (PointRend I minus L), using shared paired group draws for the geometry differences and reporting the difference in AP effects. A nonzero paired interaction can support effect modification on these tested score heads. Support for the writer across both scores requires the positive gate under each. An interval crossing zero does not establish equivalence. Release hypothesis, code, aggregate metrics and verified closure, keeping raw masks, annotations, features and weights private.
