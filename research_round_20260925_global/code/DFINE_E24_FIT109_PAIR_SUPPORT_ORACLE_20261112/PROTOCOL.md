# D-FINE FIT109 fixed-point action support oracle

Companion to the direct paired utility experiment. Reuse the exact already accepted, label-free FIT109 captured points and candidate stream; 109 distinct source proxies, native top100, 194,640 original pairs across 6,167 candidates. Neither model training nor a learned decision is evaluated. No new detector inference, raw pixels, probabilities, DEV, official TEST, AP, or threshold fitting.

Use the original stock .50 nonignored continuous valid fixed matches, including any unsupported eligible row. Keep raw candidate, matching, original mask area P, overlap I, and exact source-polygon area T. Label-free endpoint identities and pairing are sealed before truth. Match each endpoint to fractional coverage of its original native unit cell. Keep the same endpoint support in all modes and up to two times min(8,floor(P/10),number of pairs) total flips. Compare raw, exact optimum over fixed disjoint original pairs with balanced sign and optional no-op, exact optimum with original endpoints rematched and balanced counts, and exact optimum with arbitrary add/delete counts. Within the latter fixed sign counts, maximizing added truth coverage and minimizing removed coverage maximizes continuous IoU. Unsupported rows stay raw. Use exact comparisons and choose no extra edits at ties.

Report group-equal and pair-equal continuous IoU, raw and each oracle strict .75/.90, positive matched gains, and counts of strict wins opened by removing pairing vs removing equal-area constraints. The original fixed-pair oracle must replay the prior aggregate and transitions of strict counts. These are truth-only geometric limits on the detector-training-visible cohort, not deployable edits or AP; the direct learner's protocol is not changed.
