# DEV705 minimum cumulative squared-distance witness protocol

Freeze this design before opening the candidate witness counts. The population is the historical HRSID author TRAIN-derived DEV705 split, which has already supported model development. The original RF-DETR center5202 predictions and occupancy-head seeds 5217–5219 are frozen. Reuse closed B5 sparse F/I and learned bounded L records without training, detector forwards, reference reads or changes to archived files. First verify actual process closure, observed receipts, bound bytes, complete native identities, support coordinates and signs, original scores and ranks, identical budgets, matching caps, and all roster counts using the original label-free archive reader.

For each policy X in {F,L,I}, define C(X) as the unrestricted minimum sum of squared native-grid distances over bijections between that policy's fixed chosen addition and removal sets. The edge cap is the largest squared distance in the original accepted F pairs. Test feasibility for I's fixed endpoints using all cap-legal edges. W chooses I only when k>0, no perfect capped matching exists, and C(I)≤C(F) and C(I)≤C(L). Otherwise W selects L. F is a subset of an unrestricted optimal full assignment, so its original distance sum must equal C(F). L's capped realized sum may exceed C(L) and cannot be substituted. A candidate with a zero budget keeps its original mask. Unchanged slots remain part of all denominators.

Stage 1 reads no labels. Close the full 705 image, 67,386 mapped, 42,325 area-eligible and 40,897 supported candidate census. The separate head counts for positive budgets and swaps per side are (40,316,303,285), (40,512,307,976) and (40,571,306,721). Report all partitions including ineligible, unsupported and zero-budget, and each observed joint truth pattern of cap feasibility and both cost comparisons in each realized budget. Missing combinations are zero. Count genuine W≠L masks on unique image/rank identities, taking a union across heads with the original source-prefix groups. At least 20 changed candidate identities across at least eight groups is a necessary prelabel gate. Below that threshold record the closure and stop without scoring. Passing it does not establish matched-hull coverage.

Only after qualified Stage 1 closure use the original DEV705 reference and all 1,782 annotations, including all original prediction slots. Preserve the original 1,778-annotation empty-raster sensitivity. Fixed continuous geometry uses all 1,461 original valid native .50 matches in 27 acquisition prefix groups, including unchanged rows. Average the three head effects within each hull, then within each prefix, then equally across prefixes. Use 4,096 paired group bootstrap draws with PCG64(5217). W−L is the primary contrast. Report per-head AP and means, changes in geometry, and the original comparators. Require at least 20 original eligible changed matches in eight or more groups for a directional decision. The original threshold is a mean AP change of at least +0.30 points with a strictly positive 95% continuous-IoU lower bound, or its symmetric negative criterion. Preserve null and failed gates. This protocol provides neither a new test set nor external transfer evidence. Historical reserves and Umbra are unavailable for selection or threshold tuning.
