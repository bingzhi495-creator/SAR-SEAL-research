# FIT-only out-of-fold action alignment check

Lock before regeneration and do not open any gate path, labels or deployed result. Bind the actual closed paired fit result and independent readback. Recompute the five identical complementary FIT preparation fits only and demand exact ordered out-of-fold probabilities and deployed bank digest parity. Original FIT author references are used only to reconstruct the same fitting packets and train the five complementary predictors. The coverage statistics themselves use no target masks, labels, overlap gain or AP.

For each eligible original FIT candidate, call the unchanged deployed robust selector with the deterministic out-of-fold occupancies. Compare the resulting static-relative signed vector to the old and deployment-aligned training banks under the same k. For a nonstatic action, compute exact bank membership, nearest bank squared XOR distance and residual squared fraction outside the training bank span using an SVD relative tolerance of 1e-10. Report candidate-equal means and quantiles among nonstatic writes, and group-equal means over all original eligible groups after assigning zeros at the one static write and aggregating candidates within images, then images within exact decoded groups. Report bank structural rank, nonstatic counts, and old/new action overlap. The aligned bank includes every nonstatic out-of-fold robust action by design. This check measures the realized change in FIT training geometry and cannot establish source-gate or external accuracy.
