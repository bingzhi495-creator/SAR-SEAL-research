# Single-forward query fusion as an ordinary baseline for seed ensemble

Hypothesis. At least some of the three-seed boundary gain can be reproduced by different mask queries from one normal RF-DETR forward. Use only the original seed 5202 native 36-chip, 2,620-candidate outputs and its closed capture and independent acceptance from the first contour fusion experiment. Do not read either other seed candidate or plane stream or the cross-seed assignment. Keep all candidates and confidence scores for COCO. Existing three-seed performance on the same data is a descriptive higher-compute reference only.

Build disjoint candidate groups in native rank order from mask IoU >=.85. Each unassigned nonempty mask begins a group, and all remaining masks reaching .85 are assigned to it; keep them as candidates for scoring. Eligible anchor is group representative of area [32,1024), with admitted 144×144 logits. Require two descendants with admitted logits and distinct query IDs. Choose the first two in original rank order. Count and report support on all candidates and small fixed matches. Outside this support, all modes retain the original mask. No data-dependent search of threshold, number or donor order.

Use original native two-pixel Chebyshev band and cap from the existing contour scorer. Capped hard-majority disagreements define the per-candidate addition and deletion counts. Use identical counts in a classification-score-weighted soft probability vote (MNC-style control) and an anchor-only probability control. When capping disagreements, use abs(weighted mean-.5) and row-major tie order; it is also the ranking score for weighted soft edits. Reuse the exact native sign verification and fixed continuous source-polygon scoring. Prepare all mask edits without label access, close the variant stream and scope receipt before reading FIT GT. Full cohort stock COCO AP, AP75, AP90 and AR100, plus 69 fixed small-match continuous IoU and folds, reuse the first contour experiment rules. Report duplicate group occupancy, edit and match coverage, cost, exact donor identity ties and differences from the external three-forward scores.

Weighted fusion only passes if +.50 mask AP points vs raw, +.30 vs equal-count anchor, AP75 retained, and both fixed continuous IoU contrasts positive in at least two of three source folds. Do not treat the ordinary fusion as a proposed innovation, even if successful. The 36 chips were used in the prior experiment and by detector training. No new forward, optimization, DEV or TEST.
