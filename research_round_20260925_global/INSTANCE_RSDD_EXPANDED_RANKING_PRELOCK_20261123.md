# Equal-budget ranking on expanded native boundary support

Instance-RSDD's same-support masked writeback showed a positive ranking difference on Gaofen-3. The full PointRend paste nevertheless had 0.211 points higher AP than our original natural procedure. The prior mask intervention localized much of the PointRend effect to a change affecting pixels in the original native support, while that intervention jointly changed the number and sign of edits. Here the sole expanded support E is the union of all eligible pixels in the two original signed morphological layers inside the same clipped detector box, with the original integer-coordinate inclusion convention. S is the former at-most-32-per-side uncertainty-selected endpoint set. E contains S and uses no additional boundary depths. [Original ranking comparison](INSTANCE_RSDD_POINTREND_TRANSFER_ACTUAL_20261122.md) · [Mask intervention](INSTANCE_RSDD_POINTREND_SUPPORT_FACTOR_ACTUAL_20261123.md)

Fix the HRSID-trained RF-DETR center5202 E24, the two original source-only fitted heads including occupancy seed 5217, all 740 Instance-RSDD test images, and the 73,226 ordered native candidates with their boxes and scores. Use the earlier eligible native mask area [32,1024) and nondegenerate box. At every candidate inherit the archived natural occupancy swap count k, including k=0. All controlled arms independently select exactly k native background additions and k native foreground removals. Rank additions by descending foreground probability and removals by ascending probability. All new arms break probability ties by ascending native row-major index. Keep the original S features and probabilities in E. For additional E points, use the original 14 occupancy features without refitting. Reuse the PointRend adaptation's same four-step 224 × 224 RoI inference and native probability interpolation, including zero padding outside the RoI. Record pool size and realized use outside S and outside the center-defined RoI. All controls use a common edit budget, with point prediction count and runtime reported separately.

Full 740 and TerraSAR-X are companion views; retain the earlier image deduplication sensitivity. Report official mask AP, AP75, AP90, AR100, all metric fields, original native fixed-match continuous polygon IoU, FP/FN by truth area, and paired acquisition bootstrap intervals using the earlier 4096 PCG64(5217) draws. Main decision is L_E − B_E. A positive primary AP difference and a positive lower geometry interval support a ranking advantage on E for this frozen recipe. Report L_E − L_SN, B_E − B_S and their difference at equal budget, plus L_E − P for practical context even if the ranking result is negative. There is one E definition, one prior pair of heads and no optimization on the already examined target release. This follow-up can strengthen or limit direct-comparator evidence. It cannot be an independent dataset replication.

PointRend uses adaptive sparse point prediction to populate its refined RoI grid. Its controlled arm here ranks the resulting learned probabilities, followed by a shared native writeback. BPR has already evaluated the geometric upper bound of wider boundary bands, SharpContour predicts point membership along contours, and RefineMask progressively restricts boundary refinement. The new experiment evaluates the interaction of an exact editable range and a fixed-budget native ranking in this SAR carrier.
