# Common pixel-center support and PointRend padding control on Instance-RSDD

The previous full two-layer menu E used the original integer-coordinate box test. In Gaofen-3, rendered PointRend chose 25.18% of its dropped pixels outside the box when positions were subsequently measured at pixel centers. The corresponding share for occupancy was 3.41%. The comparison held support and total edits fixed, but used different scores, including the standard zero-padded PointRend interpolation. This follow-up tests whether the ranking contrast persists when every actual edit is centered inside the box and the PointRend interpolation extends the border sample rather than inserting zeros. [Previous comparison](INSTANCE_RSDD_EXPANDED_RANKING_ACTUAL_20261123.md)

Use the same original HRSID-trained RF-DETR carrier, weights and source fits, the full 740-image admitted Instance-RSDD author split and its 73,226 fixed native predictions. Reuse archived E made from the original two signed layers, area [32,1024) and original clipped nondegenerate box. Define C as E restricted to x0 ≤ x+0.5 < x1 and y0 ≤ y+0.5 < y1; use original FP32 box values and exactly representable native half-integer coordinates. Fix each candidate's common k_C=min(k, |C_add|, |C_drop|) from the original archived k. Produce k_C additions and k_C removals, with shared row-major tie order and score/candidate preservation. Candidates with k_C=0 retain their raw masks. Report clipped candidate and swap counts. C versus the previous E changes the support and may also change the realized count for those candidates.
