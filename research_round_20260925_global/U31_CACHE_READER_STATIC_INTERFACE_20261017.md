多GT单图入口：未来Root实际`prepare`后，按预先固定规则选`reader.records`中首条`G>=2`的`dataset_index`，调用一次`load(index)`即可取得同图全部G组监督；无需每艘船重复构造图像或大主干。此处不指定实际index/image_id，因为本轮未读人口metadata。资源pilot若只取其中两条，须明确其范围，不称全部G或全部100查询已验证。

## 全部100条M：FIT与DEV不能混淆

FIT N13 writer实际保存`matched_M200=M[:,query_tensor]`：[cache_fit:231](https://github.com/bingzhi495-creator/SAR-SEAL-research/blob/602f859334385b5ae4cd24ea13ee268f2a713735/research_round_20260925_global/multimodal_data_pilot_20260928/native_geometry_paired_mask_readout_preparation_20261009/N13_sameK_region_point_refinement_source_20261013_01/cache_fit.py#L231)。更早FIT N07 packet仅含采样F/匹配e/点L/GT等，没有M字段：[FIT cache_fit:318](https://github.com/bingzhi495-creator/SAR-SEAL-research/blob/602f859334385b5ae4cd24ea13ee268f2a713735/research_round_20260925_global/multimodal_data_pilot_20260928/native_geometry_paired_mask_readout_preparation_20261009/N07_frozen_E24_original_detail_point_refinement_source_20261012_01/cache_fit.py#L318)。N16 FIT也未保存原全100 M。**因此不能声称当前FIT已存完整100-query输入接口；严禁L下采样、复制G条或补0冒充M。**

DEV则有真实原字段：N07 `evaluate.py` [344行](https://github.com/bingzhi495-creator/SAR-SEAL-research/blob/602f859334385b5ae4cd24ea13ee268f2a713735/research_round_20260925_global/multimodal_data_pilot_20260928/native_geometry_paired_mask_readout_preparation_20261009/N07_frozen_E24_original_detail_point_refinement_source_20261012_01/evaluate.py#L344)保存schema `N07-original-detail-all100-native-fields-v1`、`native_fields={F256,e0,M200,final_class_logits}`和`prediction_query_ids`。公开[DEV reader:104](https://github.com/bingzhi495-creator/SAR-SEAL-research/blob/602f859334385b5ae4cd24ea13ee268f2a713735/research_round_20260925_global/code/N28_FIXED24_DEV_EVAL_DRAFT_20261017_01/dev_reader.py#L104)明确M形状`[1,100,200,200]`；配套N16 bundle/shared提供真实GPU自然序L。该API为`prepare(torch,c,context)`，`load(index)`返回自然序`e [100,256]/M [100,200,200]/L [100,H,W]`及独立输出序`query_ids`。它不读GT且只锁187 DEV人口，不能拿来充当FIT训练数据。

公开[N16 DEV原图接口:882](https://github.com/bingzhi495-creator/SAR-SEAL-research/blob/602f859334385b5ae4cd24ea13ee268f2a713735/research_round_20260925_global/code/N16_FIXED_E24_ALL100_SCOUT_SHARD_GPU_UNBOUND_20261014_01.py#L882)有`einsum('bqc,bchw->bqhw',e,F)`与真实保存M逐位相等的检查；这不证明N13 F与N16 e的跨cache组合已等价，也不等于已有真实M归档。**Root已选择直接补采真实M100，不采用L下采样、0填充或F/e重建路径。**

## query与S0序列必须另行对齐

1. FIT的`row.query_ids`是GT顺序；N16 `natural_query_ids`是张量自然0..99轴。上述N07/N13/N16 FIT packet/L-carrier不存实际`prediction_query_ids`或原S0预测字典；`native_class_logits`也不等于S0。后述N22的32图补采包另有真实export/S0映射，不能推广为全部741图都有。
2. DEV原N07 packet有`prediction_query_ids`；N16 shared同时存`natural_query_ids`与`native_export_query_ids`：[937行](https://github.com/bingzhi495-creator/SAR-SEAL-research/blob/602f859334385b5ae4cd24ea13ee268f2a713735/research_round_20260925_global/code/N16_FIXED_E24_ALL100_SCOUT_SHARD_GPU_UNBOUND_20261014_01.py#L937)。原native预测字典和`native_query_score_factors`另存并由原report引用：[N07 report:385](https://github.com/bingzhi495-creator/SAR-SEAL-research/blob/602f859334385b5ae4cd24ea13ee268f2a713735/research_round_20260925_global/multimodal_data_pilot_20260928/native_geometry_paired_mask_readout_preparation_20261009/N07_frozen_E24_original_detail_point_refinement_source_20261012_01/evaluate.py#L385)。native-field packet本身没有原S0列表。
3. 实际官方导出为`softmax(class) -> topk(100,sorted=False) -> qids -> L[qids]`，S0还乘原mask quality，不是简单按最终分数降序，也不能默认自然序：[export_original:367](https://github.com/bingzhi495-creator/SAR-SEAL-research/blob/602f859334385b5ae4cd24ea13ee268f2a713735/research_round_20260925_global/multimodal_data_pilot_20260928/native_geometry_paired_mask_readout_preparation_20261009/shared_readout_objective_control_source_20261010/train.py#L367)。对原预测第j条必须取其真实`query_id=q`对应的e/M/L；输出沿原字典顺序，仅替换segmentation，原score/category/image_id/query_id逐字段保留。不能按j误取自然L[j]。
