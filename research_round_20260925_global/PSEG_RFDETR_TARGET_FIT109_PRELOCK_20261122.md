# Target FIT109 occupancy head on the frozen PSeg-trained RF-DETR

The earlier HRSID-trained point head improved over dynamic attraction at the same count on PSeg-SSDD DEV187, but reduced AP and continuous IoU relative to the native carrier. A label-conditioned calculation found 2.246 points of continuous IoU headroom at the same endpoints and count. This test asks whether fitting the same head on target training examples improves the native masks at that fixed edit count. No replacement by content, confidence, labels or support coverage. Report the retained subset counts and FIT–DEV exact-ID and decoded-RGB disjointness. No threshold, budget or output selection on DEV187 and no official TEST evaluation in this protocol.

DEV187 has historical exposure. This is a trained-on-target comparison of point-head recipes for one frozen detector. Any positive outcome requires seed repeats and independent acquisition evidence before supporting a broad portability or TGRS submission claim.
