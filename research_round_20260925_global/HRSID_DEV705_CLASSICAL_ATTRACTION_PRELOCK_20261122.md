Predeclare the fixed sensitivity excluding those four annotations and retaining the 705 images. Reproduce all original raw COCO metrics using the same category and evaluator. Report AP, AP50, AP75, AP90, small-object AP and AR100 for the same-count comparisons with dynamic and static controls.
