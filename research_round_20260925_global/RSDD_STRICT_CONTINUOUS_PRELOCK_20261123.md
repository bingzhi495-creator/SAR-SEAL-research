# Strict-threshold Instance-RSDD AP under raster and continuous polygon overlap

Frozen detector 5203 gained mean AP against dynamic attraction at the exact same candidate edit counts on Gaofen-3, with lower AP90 at each occupancy-head seed. The corresponding 5202 contrast was positive in original raster AP90. A high mask-IoU threshold can respond strongly to representing the same annotation as a raster instead of a polygon. This study tests measurement sensitivity with the same complete candidate masks, order, confidence, quota and annotation file on each pair of evaluations. No training, inference, candidate selection, threshold selection or mask rewriting occurs. ## Same-support comparison and strict-threshold audit

Replace only the detection–truth IoU matrix. Treat each foreground native pixel as the unit square [column,column+1] by [row,row+1]. Intersect the run-based union with the original valid annotation polygon clipped to the image. Compute the continuous IoU denominator from those exact areas. Preserve original COCOeval score sort, area metadata and predicted raster area for bins, ignore/crowd semantics, confidence thresholds, maxDets, matching and accumulation. Use the original 13 reported scores, including AP90. The literal 0.90 threshold includes equality as in COCOeval. A pair can bypass exact overlap only when a conservative separate area and bounding-box upper bound proves IoU below the lowest evaluated threshold of 0.50. Retain a readback of exact geometry and influential below-bound checks. [COCO evaluator](https://github.com/cocodataset/cocoapi/blob/8c9bcc3cf640524c4c20a9c40e89cb6a2f2fa0e9/PythonAPI/pycocotools/cocoeval.py)

Reproduce original raster metrics with all original annotations. For the comparison, retain invalid, empty, unsupported or crowd annotations in the same role in both common-support calculations as explicitly ignored under evaluation preparation and keep their original raster overlap. Log the exclusions and use noncrowd matching semantics for any originally noncrowd excluded item. Do not alter or repair rings. Report original raster and common-support raster separately if they differ. Positive-support polygons must be finite, noncrowd, valid, positive after canvas clipping, and have nonempty reproducible original COCO raster coverage. The original raster remains the official metric. This sensitivity analysis measures differing representations of the same labels.

The primary effect is the change in the within-detector AP90 contrast for occupancy minus dynamic when moving from common-support raster to continuous overlap, averaged across the three heads. Report both levels of the contrast and differences between them, one row per head. Use the same 0.90, all-area, maxDets=100 greedy matching outcomes to enumerate paired TP, FP and ignore transitions and changed truth assignment. Rebuild unweighted AP90 from this exact unignored score-ordered event list. For intervals, frequency-weight the fixed ordered sequence and true-instance totals by 4,096 paired resamples of original groups (PCG64 seed 5217), preserving deterministic ties. In the primary and test sensitivities groups are the accepted author acquisition groups. In reserve sensitivities use the original sensor/calendar-date blocks. Apply the same resample to all arms and both representations. Report percentile 95% intervals on the arm contrasts and their difference. An interval containing zero leaves that population-level direction unresolved. Treat individual head intervals as sensitivity summaries. No pooled cross-carrier significance or comparison to human delineation is implied. A direction change only under the representation swap is evidence about the measurement, and a retained direction does not identify what part of the model caused the underlying shape difference.
