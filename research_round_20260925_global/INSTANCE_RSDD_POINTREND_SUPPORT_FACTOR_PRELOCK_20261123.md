## Analysis

Score N, B, H10, H01, and P with the prior official COCO mask evaluator and original annotation treatment. Compare the original N, B, and P scores against their published values. Primary estimates are the two conditional AP changes when substituting P outside S, H01−B and P−H10, and the two when substituting P on S, H10−B and P−H01. Report the interaction P−H10−H01+B and both order-averaged contributions. The interaction and conditional effects describe these four particular masks and preserve COCO rematching. Report AP50, AP75, AP90, AR100, official threshold-specific recall and the reach versus interpolation components of the observed P−B AP gap. A rank-preserving change in the mask can change which candidate matches a hull.

On native N fixed matches use the previous continuous polygons, area eligibility, group weighting and 4,096 paired acquisition resamples. Report the same mask contrasts in continuous IoU with intervals. Decompose P−B continuous IoU into realized overlap and predicted area terms using the two original unions. Count empty masks and retain all original candidate scores. The comparisons localize the effect of PointRend's realized writeback relative to the original endpoint restriction. Changes on S can alter both the number and the sign ratio of edits. The observed differences cannot assign unique effects to budget and net area. This is a retrospective mechanism analysis with previously frozen masks, and the target results do not qualify a new deployable policy.

RefineMask already restricts the successive prediction to a boundary region and BPR reassembles refined boundary patches. PointRend learns an adaptive sparse rendering. The present experiment measures the effect of the specific native uncertainty-selected endpoint constraint on a frozen SAR carrier. It does not claim a new boundary replacement primitive.
