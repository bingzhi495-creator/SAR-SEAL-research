# Signed-side test of learned occupancy and static spatial attraction

Learned occupancy is below the matched static and dynamic spatial-attraction adaptations on the previous 342-image UEIKAP evaluation. Test whether its shortfall is concentrated in one signed side of the native boundary, using a new factorial on the closed predictions. The UEIKAP population and prior rank comparisons are already exposed. HRSID DEV705 and PSeg-SSDD TEST232 provide independent data cohorts with a shared D-FINE carrier for a same-carrier comparison across source and external data. Both were previously evaluated. This protocol fixes the new contrasts before the hybrids are scored. None of the three cohorts is a new prospective holdout for the paper. [UEIKAP result](UEIKAP_ADDITIONAL_FAMILIES_ACTUAL_20261123.md) · [HRSID result](HRSID_DEV705_CLASSICAL_ATTRACTION_ACTUAL_20261122.md) · [PSeg-SSDD result](PSEG_TEST232_CLASSICAL_ATTRACTION_ACTUAL_20261122.md)

Let S denote static attraction on the original mask and L the frozen learned occupancy ranking. Use the existing static and learned policies at the exact per-candidate exchange count realized by the archived dynamic attraction adaptation. The first letter specifies which policy chooses additions and the second which chooses removals. Use only the original two signed supports and realize SS, LS, SL and LL. Preserve archived SS and LL exactly. Also retain the original native and dynamic results as context. The published pixel-swapping method uses distance-weighted context and iterated exchanges. The present static and dynamic operators are adaptations to the native ship-mask support. We do not recombine an adaptive dynamic trajectory. [Atkinson, original paper](https://doi.org/10.14358/PERS.71.7.839)

The four factorial arms keep the original candidates, scores, masks before editing, actual separate addition/removal counts, and area. Candidates with zero budget use their native masks. Recheck the signed sets, exact original masks and emitted hybrids before opening geometry labels. No new detector or head forwards, fitting, threshold changes or target-selected policy. Original matching, geometry and image ordering are tied to each archived score.

The principal UEIKAP result retains all 528 released hulls in 13 acquisition families, including zeros for unmatched and unchanged hulls. Use the 121 original native matches as a secondary sensitivity. Use the original 1,447 eligible matches and 27 source-prefix groups on HRSID DEV705 and the 293 eligible matches and 143 decoded-image groups on PSeg-SSDD TEST232, with the published duplicate-exclusion sensitivity where available from the existing scorer. The D-FINE arms report fixed-roster COCO mask AP and the same standard components under the original raster rules. UEIKAP labels are case-focused, so report released-hull geometry and coverage. Group identities change across datasets. Present the side-effect estimates within their respective cohorts.

For a matched hull let J be the continuous truth intersection and T the area of its truth polygon. The two prespecified side estimates are the group-equal changes 100 times (J(LS) minus J(SS))/T and 100 times (J(SL) minus J(SS))/T. Retain zero effects for unchanged matched hulls and, on UEIKAP, all originally unmatched released hulls. With equal predicted area verify J(LL) plus J(SS) minus J(LS) minus J(SL) has residual at most 1e-7 square pixels per evaluated match. Also report all arm mean polygon IoU, LL versus SS, and hybrids against each pure arm. AP and IoU ratios need not be additive.

Draw 4,096 paired group bootstrap replicates with PCG64 seed 5217. Report 97.5% marginal percentile intervals for the two normalized intersection effects (quantiles 1.25/98.75), which give simultaneous coverage of at least 95% within each cohort. Report ordinary paired 95% intervals for the direct addition-minus-removal contrast and secondary IoU comparisons. Count actual changed positions for each side among the original eligible matches and report the represented groups. Require at least 20 such matches in at least eight groups for a side-level decision. A localized dominant deficit requires adequate coverage on both sides, an upper marginal bound below zero on the worse side, and a direct side-difference interval excluding zero in that direction. Report all side outcomes, exact decomposition and comparison groups. A one-sided interval crossing zero alone does not establish an absence of effect. On UEIKAP include group direction and leave-one-acquisition sensitivity.

The factorial is an analysis of the observed ranking difference at a fixed edit budget. Report every arm, including hybrids that do worse than either pure arm and failures of the side decision. These historically exposed panels are for mechanism diagnosis and guide independently specified tests on future acquisitions. They do not validate a policy selected from their results.
