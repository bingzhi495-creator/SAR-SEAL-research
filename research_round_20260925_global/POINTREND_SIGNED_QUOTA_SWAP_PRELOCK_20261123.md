On the historical 583 Gaofen-3 images, the frozen same-carrier PointRend whole-RoI paste led capped occupancy by 0.211 mask AP. The previous support splice held the outside mask fixed and changed the edit positions and counts inside the support together. On the extra 74 images from 13 parents, the intact whole-RoI PointRend scored below capped occupancy. This study asks whether occupancy predicts better edit locations when the stronger whole-RoI policy supplies the exact separate addition and deletion counts for every candidate. The PointRend adaptation uses adaptive mask subdivision, while BPR has previously established local patch refinement as a method class. The new experiment isolates location choice at actual signed edit counts.
